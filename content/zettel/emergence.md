The most surprising feature of a well-maintained [[zettelkasten|Zettelkasten]] is that it begins to *surprise you*. Ideas you recorded years apart suddenly appear adjacent. A connection you didn't intend becomes the thesis of an essay.

This is emergence: structure that arises from the system, not from you. It only happens with [[slow-accumulation|slow accumulation]] — you cannot force it. The system must reach a critical mass of [[linking-ideas|linked ideas]] before it starts thinking alongside you.
