The link is the unit of insight. When you connect two notes, you are making a claim: these ideas are related in a way worth remembering. Every link is a micro-argument.

Over time, highly-linked notes reveal themselves as load-bearing concepts in your thinking — ideas that connect to many others. The [[slip-box-structure|structure of the slip-box]] makes this visible. What looks like note-taking is actually map-making.
