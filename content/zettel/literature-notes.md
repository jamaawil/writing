Literature notes are what you write while reading — brief records of what an author says, in your own words, with a reference to the source. They are not highlights. They are not copies.

The discipline is paraphrase: you must translate the author's words into yours. That translation is where [[reading-as-conversation|reading becomes a conversation]]. Literature notes feed into [[permanent-notes|permanent notes]] — they are the raw material, not the finished product.
