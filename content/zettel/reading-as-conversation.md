Passive reading leaves no trace. Active reading is a dialogue — you talk back. You write what the author's argument made you think, what you agree with, what you'd push back on, and what question it raises that the text doesn't answer.

[[literature-notes|Literature notes]] are the transcript of that conversation. The goal is not to remember what the author said but to find out what *you* think in response. Reading without writing is hearing without listening.
