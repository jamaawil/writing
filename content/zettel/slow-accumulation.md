A slip-box is not built in a sprint. The value compounds over years, not weeks. Each note added increases the number of possible connections exponentially — a system of 1,000 notes has not 1,000 but potentially millions of relationships.

This is why consistency beats intensity. Ten notes a week for three years is worth more than 500 notes in a month. The [[emergence|emergent insights]] only appear when the system has had time to accumulate density. Patience is a design requirement, not a virtue.
