A slip-box (German: *Zettelkasten*) is a system of [[permanent-notes|permanent notes]] connected by explicit links. Unlike a filing system organized by topic, a Zettelkasten is organized by connection — ideas link to other ideas, forming a web that grows smarter as it grows larger.

The method was developed by sociologist Niklas Luhmann, who used it to write over 70 books. The insight was that the value is not in any single note, but in the [[emergence|emergent structure]] that arises from linking them.
