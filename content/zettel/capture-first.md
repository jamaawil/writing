The enemy of capture is curation. When you try to decide while reading whether something is worth keeping, you interrupt both the reading and the capturing. The result is you do neither well.

Capture first, evaluate later. Fleeting notes can be messy. [[literature-notes|Literature notes]] can be rough. The question of whether something belongs in your [[zettelkasten|Zettelkasten]] is a question for the processing session, not the reading session. [[slow-accumulation|Slow accumulation]] requires a low barrier to capture.
