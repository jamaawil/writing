A permanent note is a fully formed idea written in your own words — not a quote, not a summary, but your own synthesis. It should be understandable without context and stand on its own.

The test: could you hand this note to a stranger and have them understand the idea? If not, you haven't finished thinking yet. Permanent notes emerge from [[literature-notes|literature notes]] once you ask what an idea *means* and how it connects to what you already know.
