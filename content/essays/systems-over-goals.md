Goals are useful for pointing direction. They tell you what winning looks like. But they are a poor mechanism for actually producing results.

The problem with goals is that they create a binary relationship with success: you either hit the goal or you don't. They offer no guidance about what to do on any given Tuesday. And they are fueled by motivation, which is unreliable.

Systems are different. A system is a set of repeating behaviors you run regardless of how you feel. It operates below the level of motivation and willpower. It doesn't care about your emotional state that morning.

The person who wants to write a book and the person who sits down to write 300 words every day before breakfast are not pursuing the same strategy. One is hoping. The other is building.

Replace the goal with the system. Let the outcomes take care of themselves.
