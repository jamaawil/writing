Suzette Grillot is a scholar of international relations and co-author of The International Arms Trade, which examines the global structures, regulations, and political economy governing the sale and transfer of conventional weapons between states and non-state actors. The book analyzes how the arms trade has evolved since the end of the Cold War, tracing the role of major arms-exporting states, the challenges of international arms control efforts, and the ways the trade fuels conflicts in fragile states and regions around the world. Her broader scholarship addresses international security, non-proliferation, and the political dynamics of weapons control regimes. Her work situates the arms trade within broader debates over state sovereignty, security cooperation, and the limits of international regulatory efforts to control weapons proliferation. She has held academic and policy-focused positions addressing international security studies.
