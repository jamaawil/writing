Fanny Pigeaud is a French journalist whose reporting and co-authored book Africa's Last Colonial Currency examine the CFA franc, the currency used by more than a dozen former French colonies in West and Central Africa that remains pegged to the euro and tied to institutional arrangements dating from the colonial era. Her work, produced with economist Ndongo Samba Sylla, argues that the CFA franc system perpetuates a form of monetary dependency that constrains African states' economic sovereignty long after formal decolonization, benefiting French economic and political interests at the expense of African development. She has spent years reporting from West Africa on politics, economics, and the enduring structures of Françafrique. Her journalism combines investigative rigor with sustained attention to the technical mechanics of monetary policy. Her work has helped bring debates over currency sovereignty to a wider international audience.
