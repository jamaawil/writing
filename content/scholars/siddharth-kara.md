Siddharth Kara is a researcher and author whose work investigates modern forms of exploitative and forced labor within global supply chains. His book "Cobalt Red" documents brutal labor conditions in artisanal cobalt mining in the Democratic Republic of Congo, tracing how cobalt extracted under dangerous and often exploitative circumstances feeds directly into the batteries of smartphones, laptops, and electric vehicles sold worldwide. His earlier research addressed human trafficking and modern slavery more broadly, combining fieldwork and testimony from affected workers with economic analysis of the industries that depend on their labor. His work situates contemporary consumer technology and the green energy transition within a longer history of resource extraction and unfree labor in Africa. His reporting has drawn significant public attention to the human costs embedded in the global electronics and battery supply chain.
