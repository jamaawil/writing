Seymour M. Hersh is an American investigative journalist renowned for exposing major stories of government and military wrongdoing. His reporting, collected in "My Lai 4: A Report on the Massacre and Its Aftermath," broke the story of the 1968 massacre of Vietnamese civilians by US soldiers, a revelation that shook American public confidence in official accounts of the Vietnam War and earned him a Pulitzer Prize. He later broke major stories on the Abu Ghraib prisoner abuse scandal during the Iraq War, among many other national security exposés over a decades-long career. His reporting method relies heavily on cultivating deep, long-term sources within military and intelligence establishments. He remains one of the most consequential, and at times controversial, investigative journalists covering war and the national security state.
