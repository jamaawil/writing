Angela Saini is a British science journalist who writes on the intersections of race, gender, and biology. Superior: The Return of Race Science traces how discredited theories of racial biology have persisted and periodically resurfaced within scientific institutions, even after being scientifically and morally discredited. Her earlier book Inferior examined how sexist assumptions have shaped, and distorted, scientific research on sex differences. Her writing combines rigorous engagement with scientific literature and its history with clear, accessible prose aimed at a broad public. She has become an important voice in public debates over the politics of scientific authority.
