B. W. Andrzejewski was a Polish-British linguist who became one of the foremost Western scholars of the Somali language and its rich oral poetic tradition. Working closely with the anthropologist I. M. Lewis, he co-authored Somali Poetry: An Introduction, a foundational study that brought serious scholarly attention to a poetic tradition long dismissed or overlooked outside Somalia. His linguistic work helped establish the standard written orthography for Somali and laid groundwork for the academic field of Somali studies more broadly. He treated Somali oral poetry as a sophisticated literary and political form in its own right, not merely as ethnographic curiosity. His scholarship remains foundational for anyone studying Somali language, literature, or oral history.
