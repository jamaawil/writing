Anna Stavrianakis is a British political scientist who studies the arms trade and export control policy. Taking Aim at the Arms Trade examines the politics of arms control activism and the limits of regulatory frameworks in restraining weapons proliferation. Her research analyzes how states justify arms exports even to regimes with documented human rights abuses, and how advocacy movements have sought to challenge these practices. She combines close policy analysis with critical attention to the power structures underlying the global weapons industry. Her work is a key academic reference for understanding arms trade governance.
