Mabel Moraña is a Uruguayan-American literary and cultural critic and co-editor, with Enrique Dussel and Carlos A. Jáuregui, of Coloniality at Large, a major anthology that helped consolidate the field of Latin American decolonial theory by bringing together foundational essays on coloniality, modernity, and postcolonial critique specific to the Latin American context. Her editorial and scholarly work has been central to distinguishing Latin American decolonial theory, with its particular attention to the long history of Spanish and Portuguese colonialism and mestizaje, from postcolonial theory developed primarily around British and French colonial contexts in Asia and Africa. Her own research spans colonial and contemporary Latin American literature, cultural theory, and the politics of memory. She has held academic positions at multiple American universities, helping to institutionalize Latin American cultural studies in the United States. Her editorial work remains a key entry point into the broader field of coloniality and decolonial studies.
