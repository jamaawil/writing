Ruha Benjamin is an American sociologist at Princeton University whose work examines the relationship between race, technology, and social justice. Her book "Race After Technology" introduces the concept of the "New Jim Code" to describe how supposedly neutral technological systems, from algorithms to automated decision-making, can encode and deepen existing racial hierarchies while appearing objective and race-neutral. Her edited volume "Captivating Technology" gathers scholarship on the surveillance and containment of Black communities through technical systems, from policing to health care. Her work bridges STS, critical race theory, and sociology to interrogate the political stakes of supposedly value-free innovation. She has become one of the most widely cited scholars linking algorithmic bias to longer histories of racial domination.
