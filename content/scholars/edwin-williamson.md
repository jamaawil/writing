Edwin Williamson is a British scholar of Latin American literature whose biography Borges: A Life offers a detailed account of the Argentine writer Jorge Luis Borges, tracing the relationship between his personal experiences, particularly his fraught romantic life and his complicated relationship to Argentine politics, and the labyrinths, mirrors, and infinite libraries of his fiction. Williamson argues that Borges's famously abstract and metaphysical stories were more directly shaped by personal disappointment and national history than is often assumed. His broader scholarship covers the history of the Latin American novel and Spanish-language literature more generally. He has taught Spanish literature at Oxford University. His biography remains one of the most substantial English-language accounts of Borges's life and its relationship to his work.
