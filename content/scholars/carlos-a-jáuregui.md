Carlos A. Jáuregui is a scholar of Latin American literature and cultural theory whose work engages closely with decolonial thought. Coloniality at Large, co-edited with Mabel Moraña and Enrique Dussel, gathers key essays mapping the modernity/coloniality research program and its application to Latin American literature, history, and cultural studies. His scholarship examines how colonial legacies persist in Latin American cultural production and identity formation long after formal independence. He has contributed to bridging Latin American cultural studies with the broader decolonial theoretical apparatus developed by scholars such as Aníbal Quijano and Walter Mignolo. His work is an important resource for understanding decolonial theory's specifically Latin American genealogy.
