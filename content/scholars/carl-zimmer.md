Carl Zimmer is an American science journalist known for accessible, deeply researched writing on biology and evolution. Parasite Rex explores the strange and often disturbing world of parasitic organisms, using their evolutionary strategies to illuminate broader questions about adaptation, coevolution, and the boundaries of what counts as an organism's own body versus its inhabitants. His writing brings technical biological research to general readers without sacrificing scientific rigor, a hallmark of his long career covering genetics, evolution, and infectious disease. He has written for major publications and authored numerous books spanning the history and current frontiers of biological science. He remains one of the most widely read science writers working on evolutionary biology today.
