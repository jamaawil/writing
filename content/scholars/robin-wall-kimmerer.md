Robin Wall Kimmerer is a botanist and enrolled member of the Citizen Potawatomi Nation whose book "Braiding Sweetgrass" weaves together Indigenous ecological knowledge and Western plant science. She argues that traditional Indigenous relationships to land, understood through reciprocity and gift rather than extraction, offer essential correctives to a purely instrumental scientific relationship to the natural world. Trained as a plant ecologist, she brings professional scientific expertise to a work that is simultaneously a memoir, a work of natural history, and an argument for Indigenous epistemology. Her writing has reached an unusually broad general readership for a work grounded in both scientific training and Indigenous teaching. She continues to write and teach on the integration of Indigenous knowledge and environmental science.
