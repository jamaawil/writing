Alistair Horne was a British historian and journalist known for narrative histories of conflict and empire. A Savage War of Peace remains one of the most widely read accounts of the Algerian War of Independence, tracing the brutality on both sides and the war's lasting impact on French politics and society. His broader body of work covered European history and warfare, written in a literary style aimed at general readers as much as specialists. The book has been cited by policymakers, including American officials studying counterinsurgency, for its portrait of the costs of colonial war. He remains a key narrative historian of decolonization in North Africa.
