Jayati Ghosh is an Indian economist whose extensive writing on the Global South economy critiques the standard development and macroeconomic policy prescriptions imposed on developing countries by international financial institutions. Her work examines how trade liberalization, austerity, and financial deregulation have often deepened inequality and instability in the Global South rather than delivering the promised convergence with wealthier economies, with particular attention to gender, agrarian distress, and labor markets in India and beyond. She has been an influential voice advocating for alternative development models centered on public investment, employment guarantees, and regional economic cooperation among developing nations. She has served on international commissions on tax reform and economic policy while maintaining an active role as a public commentator. Her scholarship bridges rigorous economic analysis with sustained critique of neoliberal globalization's effects on the world's poorer economies.
