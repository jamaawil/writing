Amitav Ghosh is an Indian novelist and essayist whose fiction and nonfiction range across empire, migration, and ecology. The Great Derangement argues that contemporary literary fiction has largely failed to reckon with climate change, a failure of imagination he links to the same rationalist assumptions that underwrote colonial modernity. Smoke and Ashes traces the history of the opium trade and its centrality to British imperial wealth and the making of the modern world economy. His novels, including Sea of Poppies and The Shadow Lines, weave historical research into richly textured narrative fiction concerned with empire, displacement, and interconnection. He is among the most prominent literary voices linking colonial history to the contemporary climate crisis.
