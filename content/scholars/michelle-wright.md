Michelle Wright is a Black studies scholar whose book Physics of Blackness argues against linear, single-origin narratives of Black identity built around the transatlantic slave trade and the Middle Passage as the singular defining historical event of Blackness, proposing instead a more multidimensional model drawing on physics concepts like spacetime to account for the many different, geographically and historically specific ways Blackness has been constructed and experienced around the world. She contends that centering the Middle Passage narrative, while historically important, can inadvertently marginalize other Black diasporic experiences, including those in Europe, that do not fit neatly into an African-American-centered historical framework. Her interdisciplinary approach draws on physics, philosophy, and cultural theory to develop this alternative model of diasporic identity and belonging. Her work has contributed to broader debates within Black studies over how to theorize a genuinely global, rather than US-centered, conception of Blackness. She teaches African American studies at Emory University.
