Leanne Betasamosake Simpson is a Michi Saagiig Nishnaabeg writer, musician, and scholar whose book As We Have Always Done argues for Indigenous resurgence grounded in the direct practice and regeneration of Indigenous ways of life, knowledge, and land relationships, rather than in seeking recognition or reconciliation from settler colonial institutions. She contends that meaningful decolonization requires Nishnaabeg peoples to actively rebuild everyday practices of land-based living, governance, and relationship, treating resurgence as something enacted in the present rather than deferred to future political negotiation with the state. Her work moves fluidly between academic theory, storytelling, poetry, and song, reflecting her broader commitment to Indigenous intellectual traditions and forms of knowledge transmission that do not conform to conventional academic genres. She has been highly influential in Indigenous studies and settler colonial studies for this resurgence-centered approach. She continues to work across writing, music, and community-based Indigenous education.
