Andrew Pettegree is a British historian specializing in the history of print, communication, and the book. The Library: A Fragile History, co-written with Arthur der Weduwen, traces the long and often precarious history of libraries as institutions, from ancient collections through the print revolution to the digital age, emphasizing how frequently libraries have been built, lost, and rebuilt. His broader scholarship examines how the printing press transformed information, religion, and politics in early modern Europe. He brings together book history, media history, and institutional history in accessible long-form narrative. His work is a key resource for understanding libraries not as static repositories but as contested, evolving institutions.
