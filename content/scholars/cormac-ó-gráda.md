Cormac Ó Gráda is an Irish economic historian best known for his authoritative work on the Great Irish Famine and on famine as a global historical phenomenon. His book Famine: A Short History surveys episodes of mass starvation across different centuries and continents, using economic and demographic analysis to test common explanations, from food availability decline to failures of political will and market response. He is known for a rigorous, data-driven approach that often challenges received narratives, including some standard accounts of the Irish famine's causes and death toll. His work situates famine within broader debates over entitlement, government responsibility, and the political economy of food distribution. He remains one of the leading authorities on the economic history of famine.
