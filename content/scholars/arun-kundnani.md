Arun Kundnani is a British-American writer whose work examines race, security, and the politics of the war on terror. The Muslims Are Coming! traces how counterterrorism policy in the U.S. and U.K. constructed Muslim communities as a suspect population, arguing that this securitization reproduces older colonial and racial logics under a new vocabulary of extremism. His analysis connects domestic policing and surveillance to broader histories of racism and empire. He has also written on multiculturalism, radicalization discourse, and the politics of counter-extremism programs. His work is widely cited in critical security studies and studies of Islamophobia.
