George E. Mylonas was a Greek-American archaeologist whose book Eleusis and the Eleusinian Mysteries is a foundational scholarly study of the ancient sanctuary at Eleusis and the secret religious rites performed there in honor of Demeter and Persephone. Drawing on decades of archaeological excavation at the site, he reconstructed the physical and ritual history of the sanctuary across more than a thousand years, while treating the actual content of the Mysteries, which initiates were sworn never to reveal, with appropriate scholarly caution about the limits of what archaeology alone can recover. His work combined rigorous field archaeology with careful engagement with ancient literary sources describing the rites from the outside. He held long academic positions in the United States while remaining deeply engaged with Greek archaeological fieldwork. His study remains a standard reference for the archaeology and history of the Eleusinian cult.
