Adam Hochschild is an American journalist and historian known for narrative nonfiction that brings hidden histories of atrocity and resistance to a broad readership. King Leopold's Ghost recounts the brutal exploitation of the Congo Free State under Belgian King Leopold II and the international human rights campaign that exposed it, a book credited with reviving public attention to Congolese colonial history. He has also written on the abolition of the British slave trade and on World War I, consistently pairing rigorous archival research with accessible storytelling. His work has done much to popularize histories of empire and atrocity for readers outside specialist circles. He remains a leading practitioner of literary history writing on colonialism and human rights.
