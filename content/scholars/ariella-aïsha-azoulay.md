Ariella Aïsha Azoulay is a theorist, curator, and filmmaker whose work examines photography, visual culture, and the politics of imperial archives. Potential History: Unlearning Imperialism argues that the very disciplines of history and photography are structured by imperial and colonial logics, and calls for practices of unlearning that recover the alternative worlds foreclosed by imperial violence. Her earlier work on photography theorized the image as a site of civic and political relations rather than simply documentary record. She combines theoretical writing with curatorial and artistic practice, treating archives and museums as contested political spaces. Her work sits at an unusual intersection of visual theory, decolonial thought, and activist scholarship.
