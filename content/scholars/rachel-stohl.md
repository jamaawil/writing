Rachel Stohl is an American analyst specializing in security policy and the global arms trade. Her co-authored book "The International Arms Trade" surveys the mechanisms, actors, and regulatory gaps that govern the global flow of conventional weapons, examining how legal and illicit arms transfers fuel conflict and human rights abuses. She has worked with policy institutes and government bodies on questions of weapons proliferation, drone warfare, and export controls. Her research is aimed at informing both scholarly understanding and practical policy reform around arms control regimes. She remains an active voice in debates over regulating the international weapons trade.
