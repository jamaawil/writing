Ilan Pappé is an Israeli historian, one of the group known as Israel's "new historians," whose book The Ethnic Cleansing of Palestine argues, using declassified Israeli military archives, that the displacement of Palestinians during the 1948 war was not an incidental byproduct of conflict but a planned and systematic policy of expulsion carried out by Zionist military forces under what he identifies as Plan Dalet. This directly challenged the long-dominant Israeli national narrative that Palestinian flight was largely voluntary or a result of Arab leaders' calls to leave, sparking significant controversy both academically and politically. His broader scholarship continues to examine the Israeli-Palestinian conflict through a lens critical of Zionist historiography, and he has faced professional and political backlash in Israel for his positions, eventually relocating to teach in the United Kingdom. His work remains central both to the field of "new history" and to broader international debates over the Nakba and its historical interpretation. He continues to write and speak internationally on Israel-Palestine.
