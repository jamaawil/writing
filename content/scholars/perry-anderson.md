Perry Anderson is a British historian and longtime editor of the New Left Review, one of the most influential Marxist intellectual journals in the Anglophone world. His early historical work, including "Lineages of the Absolutist State," offered a sweeping comparative account of the transition from feudalism to capitalism across Europe. He has written extensively on the history of Western Marxism as an intellectual tradition and, in later books such as "American Foreign Policy and Its Thinkers," turned to a critical analysis of the ideological architects of US global strategy. His essays combine rigorous historical scholarship with polemical engagement across intellectual and political debates. He remains a major figure in the tradition of historically grounded Marxist critique.
