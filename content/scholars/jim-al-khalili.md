Jim Al-Khalili is a British-Iraqi physicist and science communicator whose book The House of Wisdom, published in the United States as Pathfinders, surveys the achievements of medieval Islamic science, from astronomy and mathematics to medicine and optics, situating this body of work as a genuine, transformative chapter in the history of science rather than a mere bridge between Greek antiquity and the European Renaissance. Writing for a general audience, he profiles individual scholars such as al-Khwarizmi and Ibn al-Haytham while explaining their specific scientific contributions in accessible detail. Alongside his popular science writing on quantum mechanics and other physics topics, he has become one of Britain's most prominent science broadcasters. His work on Islamic science aims explicitly to counter narratives that erase or minimize non-Western contributions to the history of science. He holds an academic position in physics at the University of Surrey.
