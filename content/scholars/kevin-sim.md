Kevin Sim is a British documentary filmmaker and co-author, with Michael Bilton, of Four Hours in My Lai, a detailed investigative account of the 1968 massacre of hundreds of unarmed Vietnamese civilians by American soldiers during the Vietnam War. Drawing on interviews with surviving American soldiers, Vietnamese survivors, and military investigators, the book reconstructs the massacre hour by hour and examines the subsequent military cover-up and the limited accountability that followed, with only one soldier ultimately convicted. The work grew out of an accompanying television documentary, reflecting Sim's background in investigative broadcast journalism. The book remains one of the most thorough accounts of My Lai, treating it as a case study in how military hierarchy, dehumanization of the enemy, and command failures combine to enable atrocity. It continues to be cited in discussions of war crimes accountability and the psychology of massacre.
