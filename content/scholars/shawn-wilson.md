Shawn Wilson is an Indigenous scholar, of Opaskwayak Cree ancestry, whose book "Research Is Ceremony" articulates a distinctly Indigenous research paradigm grounded in relationality rather than the detached objectivity conventionally prized in Western academic research. He argues that for Indigenous scholars, the research process itself constitutes a ceremony, one that must honor relationships among researcher, participants, land, and knowledge rather than treating them as separable objects of study. The book has become a widely used text in Indigenous studies programs for training researchers in methodologies consistent with Indigenous worldviews and ethics. His work challenges the presumed universality of Western research methods and models an alternative grounded in accountability to community and relationship. It has been influential across disciplines seeking to decolonize research practice.
