Priya Satia is a historian of the British Empire whose work traces the deep entanglement between industrial capitalism, war, and colonial violence. Her book "Empire of Guns" argues that Britain's arms industry, and the wars it required and profited from, were central rather than incidental to the Industrial Revolution, complicating triumphalist accounts of British industrial progress. Earlier work, including "Spies in Arabia," examined British intelligence operations and imperial knowledge production in the Middle East during and after the First World War. Her scholarship consistently asks how violence has been narratively obscured or justified within accounts of economic and technological progress. She writes for both academic and general audiences on the moral costs of empire.
