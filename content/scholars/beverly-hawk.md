Beverly Hawk is a scholar of media and African studies known for editing Africa's Media Image, a collection examining how Western news media have represented Africa, often through reductive frames of crisis, famine, and conflict. The volume brings together contributors analyzing the political and economic incentives shaping foreign correspondence and international news coverage of the continent. Its central argument is that media representation is not incidental but actively shapes Western policy responses and public perception of African nations. The collection remains a frequently cited reference in media studies and African studies courses examining representation and journalism. Her work contributes to broader critiques of how the Global South is narrated in international media.
