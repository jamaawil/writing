Anand Gopal is an American journalist known for deeply reported, on-the-ground accounts of the Afghanistan war. No Good Men Among the Living reconstructs the war through the intertwined experiences of a Taliban commander, an Afghan warlord, and an Afghan woman, challenging simplified American narratives of the conflict as a clash between liberators and extremists. His reporting emphasizes how U.S. intervention interacted with, and often inflamed, preexisting local rivalries and power structures. The book is widely regarded as one of the most nuanced journalistic accounts of the war's human costs. His work is frequently cited in critical assessments of American intervention in Afghanistan.
