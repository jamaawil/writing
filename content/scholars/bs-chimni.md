B. S. Chimni is an Indian international law scholar and one of the founding voices of Third World Approaches to International Law. International Law and World Order develops a Marxist-inflected critique of international legal institutions, arguing that they have historically served to entrench the interests of dominant capitalist states even while claiming universal neutrality. His work examines how international economic law, refugee law, and institutions of global governance reproduce inequalities between the global North and South. He has been influential in building TWAIL as a distinct school within international legal scholarship. His writing bridges legal theory, political economy, and postcolonial critique of global institutions.
