Alaa Al Aswany is an Egyptian novelist and dentist by training whose fiction and nonfiction have offered some of the sharpest portraits of Egyptian society under authoritarian rule. His novel The Yacoubian Building became an international bestseller for its unflinching depiction of corruption, class, and sexuality in Cairo. On the State of Egypt collects his nonfiction writing on politics under Hosni Mubarak, reflecting his role as a public intellectual and critic during the years leading up to the 2011 uprising. He has continued to write and comment on Egyptian politics from exile, maintaining a voice that blends literary craft with direct political engagement. His work is a key entry point into contemporary Egyptian social and political life.
