Ayesha Jalal is a Pakistani-American historian known for revisionist scholarship on the partition of British India. The Sole Spokesman offered a controversial reassessment of Muhammad Ali Jinnah, arguing that his demand for Pakistan functioned initially as a negotiating position for Muslim political power within a united India rather than a fixed goal of separate statehood, and that partition emerged from a complex, contingent political process rather than inevitable communal division. Her work has reshaped debates over the causes and responsibility for partition among historians of South Asia. She has continued to write on nationalism, Islam, and state formation in Pakistan and the broader subcontinent. Her scholarship remains central to any serious study of partition historiography.
