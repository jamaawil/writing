China Miéville is a British writer and scholar best known as a novelist working in speculative and weird fiction, but he also holds a doctorate in international law and has written serious academic work in that field. His book Between Equal Rights: A Marxist History of International Law argues that international law is not a neutral framework gradually approaching justice but a structure whose very form, the relation between formally equal sovereign states, has historically enabled and legitimated imperial violence. Drawing on Marxist legal theory, he traces this argument through the history of international legal doctrine from the nineteenth century onward. The book is unusual in bridging his fiction writing and his formal legal training, and it has been influential among scholars of the history and politics of international law. He remains active as both a novelist and a left political commentator.
