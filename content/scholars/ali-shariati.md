Ali Shariati was an Iranian sociologist and revolutionary intellectual whose synthesis of Marxist social analysis and Shia Islamic thought profoundly shaped the ideological currents leading to the 1979 Iranian Revolution. On the Sociology of Islam presents his effort to read Islamic tradition, and particularly the figure of the martyr, through the lens of social struggle and liberation rather than quietist orthodoxy. He lectured widely to young, politically engaged audiences in Iran, positioning Islam as a revolutionary force capable of confronting both Western imperialism and the Pahlavi monarchy. His ideas were controversial among clerical authorities even as they galvanized a generation of activists. He died in exile shortly before the revolution he helped inspire, and his influence on political Islam remains widely debated.
