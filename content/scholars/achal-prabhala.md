Achal Prabhala is an Indian writer and researcher who works on access to medicines and the politics of intellectual property in global health. His essays on vaccine equity, sharpened during the COVID-19 pandemic, examine how patent regimes and pharmaceutical monopolies restrict access to lifesaving treatments in the Global South. He coordinates the AccessIBSA project, which pushes for generic drug access across India, Brazil, and South Africa, connecting legal advocacy with public writing. His work sits at the intersection of public health, trade law, and postcolonial critique of global governance structures. He is a persistent voice arguing that pandemic preparedness is inseparable from questions of equity and ownership.
