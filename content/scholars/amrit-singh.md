Amrit Singh is a human rights lawyer whose work with the ACLU produced detailed documentation of the U.S. government's post-9/11 rendition and torture program. Her reports on rendition compiled evidence of extraordinary rendition flights, secret detention sites, and the treatment of detainees, providing some of the most rigorous public accounting of these practices. This work fed into broader legal and advocacy efforts to hold state and private actors accountable for torture and unlawful detention. Her research sits at the intersection of international human rights law and post-9/11 national security policy. It remains a key primary source for scholars and journalists studying the war on terror's legal and human costs.
