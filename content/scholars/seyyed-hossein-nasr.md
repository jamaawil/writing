Seyyed Hossein Nasr is an Iranian-American philosopher and one of the most prominent contemporary scholars of Islamic thought and the history of science in the Islamic world. His book "Science and Civilization in Islam" was an early and influential effort to present the achievements of Islamic science, medicine, and philosophy to a Western audience as a coherent, sophisticated tradition in its own right rather than a mere transmission belt between Greek antiquity and the European Renaissance. A prominent voice in traditionalist and perennialist philosophy, he has written widely on Islamic metaphysics, Sufism, and the relationship between religion, science, and the environmental crisis. He has held professorships at major American universities and served as a bridge figure introducing Islamic intellectual traditions to Western academic audiences. His extensive body of work spans philosophy, comparative religion, and the history of science.
