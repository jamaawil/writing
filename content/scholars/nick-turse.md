Nick Turse is an American investigative journalist whose book Tomorrow's Battlefield documents the rapid, largely unpublicized expansion of United States military operations across Africa in the twenty-first century, conducted primarily through the U.S. Africa Command established in 2007. Drawing on Freedom of Information Act requests, leaked documents, and on-the-ground reporting, he traces a growing network of American bases, training missions, and special operations activities across dozens of African countries, arguing that this expansion has occurred with minimal congressional oversight or public awareness despite its significant scale and implications. His earlier book Kill Anything That Moves offered a similarly investigative reassessment of the scale of American war crimes during the Vietnam War, arguing that atrocities against civilians were far more widespread and systematic than typically acknowledged in mainstream historical accounts. His journalism consistently focuses on exposing the hidden scale and mechanics of American military engagement abroad. He continues to report on U.S. military policy in Africa and elsewhere.
