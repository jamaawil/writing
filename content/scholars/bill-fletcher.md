Bill Fletcher Jr. is an American labor organizer, writer, and racial justice activist with a long history in the U.S. labor movement, including senior roles in national union federations. Claim No Easy Victories, which he co-edited, gathers reflections on the legacy of Amílcar Cabral and African liberation struggles, connecting that history to contemporary movements for racial and economic justice. His broader writing addresses the relationship between organized labor, Black politics, and international solidarity movements. He has combined decades of on-the-ground organizing experience with sustained political writing and commentary. His work bridges labor history, Pan-Africanism, and contemporary social movement strategy.
