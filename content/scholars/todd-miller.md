Todd Miller is an American journalist whose book Empire of Borders examines how the United States has exported its border security infrastructure, technology, and training well beyond its own physical borders, funding and equipping border enforcement operations in Mexico, Central America, and beyond as part of a broader strategy of pushing migration control further from American territory. He argues that this externalization of border enforcement extends American security and surveillance power into other sovereign nations while insulating the United States from direct accountability for the resulting human rights consequences faced by migrants intercepted far from the actual US border. His reporting draws on extensive fieldwork tracing border security funding, training programs, and technology exports across multiple countries. His broader journalism examines border militarization, climate migration, and immigration enforcement more generally. His work situates American border policy within a broader global pattern of border externalization by wealthy nations.
