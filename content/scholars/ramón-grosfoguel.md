Ramón Grosfoguel is a Puerto Rican sociologist associated with the "modernity/coloniality" research program alongside thinkers like Aníbal Quijano and Walter Mignolo. His work develops the concept of the "coloniality of power" to describe how racial hierarchies established under European colonialism continue to structure global economic and knowledge systems long after formal decolonization. In writing on "The Structure of Knowledge in Westernized Universities," he critiques the epistemic racism embedded in academic institutions, arguing that Western universities marginalize non-Western ways of knowing even in ostensibly global curricula. His scholarship is a central reference point within decolonial studies for understanding the persistence of coloniality within supposedly neutral institutions like the university. He has been an active participant in debates over decolonizing higher education internationally.
