Roy MacLeod is a historian of science known for his work on the institutional and imperial dimensions of scientific practice. As editor of "The Library of Alexandria: Centre of Learning in the Ancient World," he brought together scholarship reassessing the famous ancient library's role in the transmission and organization of knowledge across the Mediterranean and Near East. His broader scholarly career has examined the relationship between science and empire, particularly within the British scientific establishment and its colonial networks. He has written on the sociology and institutional history of science across a range of national and imperial contexts. His work situates the history of libraries and scientific institutions within larger questions of imperial knowledge production.
