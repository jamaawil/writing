Nnimmo Bassey is a Nigerian environmental activist, architect, and poet whose book To Cook a Continent argues that resource extraction across Africa, particularly oil extraction in the Niger Delta, constitutes a form of ongoing environmental warfare against African peoples and ecosystems conducted primarily for the benefit of multinational corporations and complicit political elites. He has been a leading figure in environmental justice organizing in Nigeria and across Africa, founding and leading organizations including Environmental Rights Action and later Health of Mother Earth Foundation, and serving as chair of Friends of the Earth International. His activism and writing connect specific local environmental struggles, particularly around Shell's operations in the Niger Delta, to broader critiques of the global political economy of fossil fuel extraction and climate change. He combines direct grassroots organizing experience with poetic and polemical writing aimed at both African and international audiences. He remains one of the most prominent African voices in global environmental justice movements.
