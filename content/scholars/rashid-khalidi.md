Rashid Khalidi is a Palestinian-American historian who held the Edward Said Chair in Modern Arab Studies at Columbia University. His book "The Hundred Years' War on Palestine" narrates the conflict as a sustained settler-colonial campaign against Palestinian national aspirations, running from the early Zionist movement through successive wars and negotiations, and draws in part on his own family's historical documents. His earlier scholarship examined the formation of Palestinian national identity and the diplomatic history of the Arab-Israeli conflict. He has combined rigorous archival history with public commentary and advocacy on Palestinian rights. His work is widely used as a counterpoint to more state-centered narratives of the conflict's history.
