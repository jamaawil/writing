Barry Blesser was an American engineer and researcher in acoustics and audio technology. Spaces Speak, Are You Listening?, co-written with Linda-Ruth Salter, develops the concept of aural architecture, examining how built and natural spaces shape human experience through sound in ways that are often overlooked relative to the visual dimensions of architecture. The book draws on acoustics, psychology, history, and cultural analysis to argue that the sonic properties of spaces, from cathedrals to concert halls to everyday rooms, are deeply consequential for how people perceive and inhabit them. His technical background in audio engineering gave the book unusual rigor in explaining the physics of sound alongside its cultural interpretation. It remains an influential text in sound studies and architectural acoustics.
