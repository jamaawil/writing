David Van Reybrouck is a Belgian historian and writer whose book Congo: The Epic History of a People offers a sweeping narrative account of Congolese history from precolonial societies through Belgian colonization, independence, dictatorship, and the wars that followed. Drawing on archival research alongside interviews with elderly Congolese eyewitnesses, he sought to tell the country's history partly through Congolese voices rather than solely through colonial and Belgian records. The book was notable for bringing the brutal history of Belgian rule under Leopold II and the colonial state to a wide popular readership, and for treating Congolese agency and resilience as central to the story rather than incidental. He has also written on democratic reform and citizen assemblies. His work bridges rigorous history with accessible, humane narrative nonfiction.
