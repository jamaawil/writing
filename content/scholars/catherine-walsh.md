Catherine Walsh is a scholar based in Ecuador whose work has been central to developing decolonial theory as both an intellectual and pedagogical project in Latin America. On Decoloniality, co-authored with Walter Mignolo, lays out a framework distinguishing decoloniality from postcolonial theory, emphasizing decoloniality's roots in Latin American and Indigenous struggles against the coloniality of power, knowledge, and being. Her work is grounded in extensive engagement with Indigenous and Afro-descendant social movements and intercultural education initiatives in the Andes. She has worked to translate decolonial theory into concrete pedagogical and institutional practice, rather than treating it purely as an academic framework. Her scholarship remains central to debates over what decoloniality means as both theory and practice.
