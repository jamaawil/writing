Ali Allawi is an Iraqi politician, scholar, and former government minister who held finance and defense portfolios in post-invasion Iraq. The Occupation of Iraq offers an insider's account of the American-led occupation and the political reconstruction that followed the 2003 invasion, combining firsthand experience with critical historical analysis of the occupation's failures. His broader writing addresses the crisis of Islamic civilization and the tensions between tradition, secularism, and modernity in the contemporary Middle East. As both practitioner and scholar he brings a rare dual perspective to the study of post-invasion Iraqi statecraft. His work is frequently cited in assessments of the occupation's political and institutional consequences.
