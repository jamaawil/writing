Glen Coulthard is a Yellowknives Dene political theorist whose book Red Skin, White Masks, its title echoing Frantz Fanon, argues that the politics of recognition, in which settler states extend limited legal acknowledgment to Indigenous rights and identity, has become a means of managing and pacifying Indigenous resistance rather than a genuine path to decolonization. He contends that meaningful Indigenous self-determination requires resurgent, land-based practices and political refusal rather than accommodation within settler legal and political frameworks that were never designed to relinquish real power. Drawing on both Fanon and Indigenous political thought, he offers a sustained critique of Canadian multicultural recognition politics specifically. His work has been highly influential in Indigenous studies and settler colonial studies internationally. He teaches political science and First Nations studies at the University of British Columbia.
