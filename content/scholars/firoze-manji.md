Firoze Manji is a Kenyan-born writer, publisher, and activist who founded Pambazuka News, an influential pan-African online platform for progressive commentary on African politics, development, and social justice. His co-edited works, including Claim No Easy Victories and The Missionary Position, are sharply critical of the international NGO and development aid sector, arguing that much of what passes for humanitarian and development work in Africa reproduces colonial relationships of dependency and depoliticizes what are fundamentally political struggles for justice and self-determination. He draws on his own experience working within and alongside NGOs to offer an insider's critique rather than a purely external one. His publishing and writing work has amplified African intellectual and activist voices often marginalized in mainstream development discourse. He remains an influential figure in pan-African media and left political thought.
