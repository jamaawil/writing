David Anderson is a British historian of Africa whose book Histories of the Hanged provided one of the most detailed and damning accounts of British counterinsurgency violence during the Mau Mau uprising in 1950s Kenya. Drawing on colonial court records and testimonies, he documented the systematic use of detention camps, torture, and mass executions by British forces, work that contributed directly to a later legal case in which surviving Mau Mau veterans sued the British government for compensation. His research helped establish the scale of colonial violence in Kenya against decades of official minimization. He has continued to write on East African history, security, and the long afterlives of colonial rule. His work exemplifies how rigorous archival history can reshape both public memory and legal accountability for imperial violence.
