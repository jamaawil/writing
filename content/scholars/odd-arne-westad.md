Odd Arne Westad is a Norwegian historian known for reorienting Cold War history away from a Washington-Moscow axis toward the Third World. His landmark book "The Global Cold War: Third World Interventions and the Making of Our Times" argues that the superpowers' ideological rivalry was fought most consequentially through interventions in Asia, Africa, and Latin America, with lasting effects on those societies. His broader work treats the Cold War as an extension of earlier imperial competition, linking decolonization directly to superpower rivalry. He has also written extensively on modern Chinese history and China's global relations. His scholarship has been influential in shifting international history toward the perspectives of formerly colonized states.
