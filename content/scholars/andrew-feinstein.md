Andrew Feinstein is a South African-British writer and former member of the South African Parliament who became a leading investigator of the global arms trade. The Shadow World exposes the corruption, bribery, and lack of accountability that pervade international weapons deals, drawing on his own experience resigning from Parliament over an arms-related corruption scandal. His work connects the arms industry to broader patterns of state corruption, conflict financing, and impunity for powerful actors. He combines insider political experience with investigative rigor in documenting an industry that operates with unusual secrecy. His writing has become a standard reference on the political economy of the arms trade.
