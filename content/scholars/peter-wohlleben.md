Peter Wohlleben is a German forester and author whose book "The Hidden Life of Trees" brought research on plant communication, cooperation, and sensory perception to a mass popular audience. Drawing on decades managing a forest in Germany, he describes trees as social organisms that share resources through fungal networks, warn one another of threats, and exhibit behaviors that resemble memory and care for offspring. The book sparked wide public interest, and some scientific pushback, over how far concepts like communication and feeling can properly be extended to plants. His writing has been influential in popularizing forest ecology and shifting public attitudes toward conservation. He continues to write and lecture on forest ecosystems and alternative approaches to woodland management.
