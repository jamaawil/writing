David Vine is an American anthropologist whose research examines the global network of United States military bases and their human and political costs. His book Base Nation surveys the hundreds of American military installations located in dozens of countries, arguing that this vast overseas footprint has often displaced local populations, most notably the Chagossians expelled from Diego Garcia, while entrenching a form of quasi-imperial presence with limited public scrutiny or debate. He combines ethnographic fieldwork with policy analysis to examine the economic, environmental, and political effects of basing on host communities. His earlier work focused specifically on the Chagos Archipelago expulsion. He has become a leading academic voice questioning the scale and necessity of the American overseas base network.
