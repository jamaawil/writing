Safiya Umoja Noble is an American scholar whose book "Algorithms of Oppression" examines how search engines and other information technologies encode and reproduce racist and sexist bias, using search results for Black women to demonstrate how supposedly neutral algorithms can perpetuate demeaning stereotypes. Her research reframes technology companies not as neutral platforms but as powerful arbiters of public knowledge whose design choices carry real social consequences. She situates her critique of search engines within a longer history of how information systems, from libraries to media, have historically marginalized Black and other minority perspectives. Her work has been influential in establishing critical algorithm studies and questions of racial bias in artificial intelligence as major academic concerns. She continues to research and advocate around technology, race, and public policy.
