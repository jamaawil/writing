Christian Bueger is a scholar of international relations whose research focuses on global maritime security, piracy, and ocean governance. His work examines how international responses to threats like Somali piracy have been organized, often through ad hoc coalitions of navies, private security firms, and international institutions, and what these arrangements reveal about the changing nature of global security governance. He pays close attention to the everyday practices and knowledge-making processes through which security problems are defined and acted upon, rather than treating security threats as simply self-evident. His scholarship connects security studies with science and technology studies and the sociology of expertise. He remains an active commentator on maritime security policy in the Indian Ocean and beyond.
