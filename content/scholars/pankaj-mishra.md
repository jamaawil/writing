Pankaj Mishra is an Indian essayist and writer known for tracing the intellectual response of Asian thinkers to Western imperial domination. His book "From the Ruins of Empire: The Intellectuals Who Remade Asia" follows figures such as Jamal al-Din al-Afghani, Liang Qichao, and Rabindranath Tagore as they grappled with colonial subjugation and searched for paths to national renewal outside Western models. In "Age of Anger" he extends this inquiry into a broader account of resentment and reaction against modernity across the globe. His essays, published widely in international outlets, combine historical narrative with sharp commentary on contemporary politics and the legacies of empire. He is one of the more prominent public intellectuals writing about decolonization and Asian modernity for a general readership.
