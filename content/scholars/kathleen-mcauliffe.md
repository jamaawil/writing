Kathleen McAuliffe is an American science journalist whose book This Is Your Brain on Parasites surveys the growing body of research on how parasites manipulate the behavior of their hosts, from fungi that control ants to toxoplasma's documented effects on rodent and, controversially, potentially human behavior. She synthesizes research across parasitology, neuroscience, and evolutionary biology to argue that host behavior manipulation is a far more widespread and significant evolutionary strategy than commonly appreciated, with implications for understanding aspects of human personality, risk-taking, and even culture. Her writing is aimed at a general audience, translating specialized and sometimes speculative scientific research into an accessible and often unsettling popular narrative. She has written extensively on medicine, science, and psychology for major American publications throughout her career. Her work has helped popularize the study of host-manipulation as a distinct area of evolutionary biology.
