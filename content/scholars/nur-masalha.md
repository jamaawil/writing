Nur Masalha is a Palestinian-British historian whose scholarship reconstructs the modern history of Palestine against the erasures of Zionist historiography. His book "Palestine: A Four Thousand Year History" argues for a deep, plural historical continuity of the land's Arab and indigenous populations against nationalist narratives that treat Jewish return as the only legitimate claim. Earlier work, including his studies of Zionist "transfer" thinking, traced the intellectual and political roots of Palestinian expulsion back into pre-1948 Zionist planning. He has also examined how biblical narratives have been mobilized as political instruments in the Israeli-Palestinian conflict. His work sits at the center of the "new history" reassessment of 1948 and remains a touchstone for scholars studying settler colonialism in Palestine.
