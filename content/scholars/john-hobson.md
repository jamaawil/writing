John Hobson is a scholar of international relations whose book The Eastern Origins of Western Civilisation argues that standard Western historical narratives dramatically understate the extent to which European development, from technology and science to trade and ideas, drew on and was made possible by prior and ongoing exchange with Islamic, Chinese, and Indian civilizations. He contends that mainstream Western historiography has constructed a myth of self-generated European exceptionalism that erases this deep Eastern contribution, a bias he traces to Eurocentric assumptions embedded within the social sciences themselves, including international relations theory. His broader scholarship critiques Eurocentrism within IR theory specifically, examining how the discipline's core concepts often naturalize Western power. His work is part of a broader "global historical sociology" project seeking to decenter Europe within accounts of world historical development. He has taught politics and international studies in the United Kingdom.
