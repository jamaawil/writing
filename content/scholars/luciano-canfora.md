Luciano Canfora is an Italian classical scholar whose book The Vanished Library reconstructs the history, and the enduring historical mystery, of the Library of Alexandria, one of the ancient world's greatest centers of learning, examining the conflicting ancient accounts of its destruction and using them as a window into how the classical world itself understood knowledge, empire, and cultural loss. Rather than offering a single definitive account of the library's fate, he treats the very uncertainty and contradiction in ancient sources as historically revealing, showing how each era retold the story of Alexandria's destruction to serve its own political and cultural purposes. His broader scholarship spans classical philology, ancient historiography, and the political uses of the classical past in modern politics. He has taught classical philology in Italy for decades. His work exemplifies a historiographical approach attentive to how historical narratives themselves are constructed and contested.
