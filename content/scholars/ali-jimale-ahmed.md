Ali Jimale Ahmed is a Somali-American scholar of comparative literature and a poet in his own right. As editor of The Invention of Somalia he assembled a landmark collection interrogating the colonial and postcolonial construction of Somali national identity, challenging simplified narratives of clan and nation. His poetry collection Daybreak Is Near reflects his standing as both a literary and scholarly voice within the Somali diaspora. His academic work moves fluidly between literary criticism, historiography, and cultural analysis of the Horn of Africa. He is a key figure connecting Somali studies to broader postcolonial and diaspora literary scholarship.
