Brent Hayes Edwards is an American literary scholar known for his work on Black internationalism and diaspora. The Practice of Diaspora examines the intellectual and literary exchanges between Black American and Francophone Black writers and activists in interwar Paris, paying close attention to the practical work of translation as a site where diasporic solidarities were built and where they also broke down. His scholarship insists on attending to the specific textual and linguistic labor involved in transnational Black political and literary movements, rather than treating diaspora as a purely abstract concept. He has also written extensively on jazz and Black experimental writing. His work is foundational to the study of Black transnational literary culture in the twentieth century.
