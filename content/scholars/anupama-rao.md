Anupama Rao is a historian whose work focuses on caste, gender, and social justice movements in modern India. The Caste Question examines the political thought and activism surrounding Dalit rights, tracing debates over untouchability, citizenship, and equality that ran through colonial and postcolonial Indian history. Her scholarship engages closely with the legacy of B. R. Ambedkar and the broader anti-caste movement, situating caste as a central rather than peripheral category for understanding Indian modernity. She brings together social history, political theory, and gender analysis in her treatment of caste politics. Her work has helped bring anti-caste thought into wider conversation with global histories of race and social hierarchy.
