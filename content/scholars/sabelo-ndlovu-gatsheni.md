Sabelo Ndlovu-Gatsheni is a Zimbabwean scholar who has become one of the leading voices extending decolonial theory into African studies. His book "Epistemic Freedom in Africa" argues for a thoroughgoing decolonization of African knowledge production, contending that formal political independence left intact colonial epistemic structures that continue to shape how Africa is studied, taught, and understood, including within African universities themselves. His work draws on and extends the "coloniality of power" framework developed by Latin American decolonial theorists, applying it specifically to African historical and institutional contexts. He has written extensively on Zimbabwean nationalism, Pan-Africanism, and the politics of knowledge in African higher education. His scholarship has been influential in South African and broader African debates over curriculum decolonization.
