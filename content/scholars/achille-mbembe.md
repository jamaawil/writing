Achille Mbembe is a Cameroonian historian and political theorist whose work has become central to contemporary postcolonial and decolonial thought. His book Necropolitics extends Foucault's biopolitics to theorize how colonial and postcolonial power operates through the administration of death, while On the Postcolony explored the entangled, often absurdist textures of power in postcolonial African states. Out of the Dark Night continues this project, reflecting on Africa's place in a global order still structured by the afterlives of slavery and colonialism. He writes across history, philosophy, and political theory, and his essays circulate widely beyond academic African studies into broader debates on race, borders, and planetary politics. Few living theorists have shaped the vocabulary of decolonial critique as much as he has.
