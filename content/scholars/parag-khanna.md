Parag Khanna is a geopolitical strategist and author who writes about the shifting balance of global power and connectivity in the twenty-first century. His book "The Future Is Asian" argues that Asian civilizations, economies, and networks, rather than a single rising China, will define the coming era. Earlier work, notably "Connectography," focuses on infrastructure, supply chains, and connective technologies as the new determinants of geopolitical power, arguing that maps of connectivity matter more than maps of political borders. He has worked as an advisor and analyst on global strategy and frequently addresses globalization, migration, and technology's reshaping of geography. His writing popularizes debates about a post-Western world order for a broad policy and business audience.
