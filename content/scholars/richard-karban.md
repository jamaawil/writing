Richard Karban is an ecologist whose research focuses on plant behavior, sensing, and communication. His book "Plant Sensing and Communication" synthesizes decades of research showing that plants can detect herbivore attack, communicate warning signals to neighboring plants, and adjust their chemical defenses in response, challenging older assumptions that plants are simply passive organisms. His work is grounded in rigorous experimental ecology rather than speculative or anthropomorphizing claims about plant consciousness. He has conducted long-term field studies, notably on sagebrush, that have become reference points in the study of induced plant defenses. His research contributes to a broader scientific reassessment of the sophistication of plant behavioral ecology.
