Sven Lindqvist was a Swedish writer whose book Exterminate All the Brutes, its title drawn from Joseph Conrad's Heart of Darkness, traces the intellectual history of European genocidal thinking about Africa and other colonized regions, arguing that the ideological groundwork for the Holocaust was substantially laid by centuries of European colonial violence and racial theorizing about "inferior" peoples that preceded and, in his argument, directly enabled Nazi genocide. Written as a combination of travel narrative through the Sahara and essayistic historical argument, the book traces how figures across European science, literature, and colonial administration developed and normalized ideas of racial extermination well before the twentieth century's most infamous genocides. His unconventional essayistic style, blending personal travel narrative with dense historical and literary argument, became a hallmark of his broader body of work examining European colonial violence and its ideological roots. His work has been influential in connecting Holocaust history directly to the longer history of European colonialism. He died in 2019, having written extensively on colonialism, war, and historical memory throughout his career.
