Ashish Kothari is an Indian environmentalist and a founding member of the environmental research and advocacy group Kalpavriksh. As co-editor of Pluriverse: A Post-Development Dictionary he helped assemble a wide-ranging collection of alternative frameworks to conventional economic development, drawing on Indigenous, ecological, and grassroots movements from across the globe. His work bridges environmental policy, biodiversity conservation, and critiques of extractive development models in India and beyond. He has been active in shaping Indian environmental law and policy debates over decades. His scholarship consistently foregrounds grassroots and non-Western alternatives to dominant development paradigms.
