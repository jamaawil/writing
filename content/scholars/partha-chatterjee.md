Partha Chatterjee is an Indian political theorist and historian, a founding member of the Subaltern Studies collective that reshaped the historiography of colonial and postcolonial South Asia. His book "The Nation and Its Fragments" develops an influential distinction between the "outer," material domain of colonial statecraft, where anticolonial nationalists conceded Western superiority, and an "inner," spiritual domain of culture where sovereignty was preserved and nationalist identity constructed. This framework has been widely taken up, and contested, as a way of understanding how anticolonial nationalisms negotiated Western modernity without simply imitating it. His later work on "political society" examines how the urban poor in postcolonial democracies engage the state outside formal citizenship. He remains one of the most cited theorists of nationalism and postcolonial democracy.
