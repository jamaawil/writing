Alice Conklin is an American historian of modern France and its empire. Her book A Mission to Civilize examines the ideology of the mission civilisatrice in French West Africa, showing how republican ideals of liberty and reason were mobilized to justify colonial rule even as they sat in evident contradiction with colonial violence and racial hierarchy. Her work traces the administrators, policies, and intellectual justifications that structured French colonial governance in the early twentieth century. She is part of a generation of historians who have pushed French imperial history to reckon more directly with its ideological self-justifications. Her scholarship is a standard reference for understanding French republican colonialism.
