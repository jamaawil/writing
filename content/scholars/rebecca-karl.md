Rebecca Karl is a historian of modern China whose work situates Chinese intellectual and political history within global and anticolonial frameworks. Her book "Staging the World" examines how Chinese reformers and revolutionaries in the early twentieth century understood their own national crisis through comparison with other colonized and semi-colonized nations, positioning China's struggles within a broader anti-imperialist world-historical narrative. Her scholarship pushes against accounts of Chinese nationalism that treat it as an inward-looking phenomenon, emphasizing instead its constitutive relationship to global anticolonial thought. She has also written on Mao Zedong and on Chinese Marxism more broadly. Her work is representative of a wider effort to write Chinese history as global history rather than as an isolated national story.
