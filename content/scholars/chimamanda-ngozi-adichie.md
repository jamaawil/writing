Chimamanda Ngozi Adichie is a Nigerian novelist and essayist whose fiction, including Half of a Yellow Sun and Americanah, has made her one of the most widely read African writers of her generation. Her novels move between Nigerian history, particularly the Biafran war, and the experience of African migrants navigating race and identity in the West. She is also known for her essay and TED talk "The Danger of a Single Story," which argues that reducing any people or place to one narrative, especially one imposed from outside, produces incomplete and often damaging understanding. Her feminist essay "We Should All Be Feminists" further extended her reach as a public intellectual beyond fiction. She writes with a clarity and warmth that has brought postcolonial and feminist concerns to a broad general readership.
