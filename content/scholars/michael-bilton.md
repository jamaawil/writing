Michael Bilton is a British investigative journalist and co-author, with Kevin Sim, of Four Hours in My Lai, a detailed reconstruction of the 1968 massacre of Vietnamese civilians by American troops during the Vietnam War. Drawing on extensive interviews with American veterans who participated in or witnessed the massacre, as well as Vietnamese survivors, the book reconstructs the event's timeline and the subsequent military investigation and limited prosecution that followed. Bilton's investigative reporting background contributed to the book's methodical, evidence-based approach to reconstructing a highly contested and traumatic historical event. The work remains a widely cited account of My Lai, valued for its detailed multi-perspective reconstruction and its examination of institutional failures in both the massacre and its aftermath. It continues to be read alongside other major investigations into American conduct and accountability during the Vietnam War.
