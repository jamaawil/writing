Prasenjit Duara is a historian of modern China known for challenging linear, nation-centered models of historical narrative. His book "Rescuing History from the Nation" argues that conventional historiography imposes the modern nation-state retroactively onto the past, obscuring the more fragmented, contested, and non-linear ways history was actually experienced and narrated. His research on Manchukuo and East Asian regionalism examines how sovereignty, empire, and modernity were negotiated outside straightforward colonizer-colonized frameworks. He has also written on the globalization of nationalism and on sustainability and transcendence as alternative categories for thinking about world history. His work is widely used in debates over how to write history beyond methodological nationalism.
