Ann Laura Stoler is an American anthropologist and historian known for her influential work on colonial archives and governance in the Dutch East Indies. Along the Archival Grain argues that colonial archives should be read not just for the facts they record but for the anxieties, uncertainties, and affective states of colonial administrators embedded in their production, treating the archive itself as an object of ethnographic study. Her broader scholarship examines race, sexuality, and intimacy as sites where colonial power was produced and contested. She has been influential in shaping how historians and anthropologists approach questions of colonial knowledge and its epistemological limits. Her work is foundational to critical colonial and archival studies.
