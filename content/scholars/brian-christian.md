Brian Christian is an American writer whose work examines the intersection of computer science, philosophy, and human values. The Alignment Problem surveys the technical and ethical challenge of ensuring artificial intelligence systems act in accordance with human intentions and values, tracing both the history of machine learning research and the growing awareness of its risks around bias, safety, and control. He writes for a general audience while engaging seriously with technical machine learning literature, making complex debates in AI safety accessible without oversimplifying them. His earlier books examined related questions about human cognition, decision-making, and what computation reveals about the mind. He has become one of the most widely read popular explainers of AI alignment and ethics.
