Tom Burgis is a British investigative journalist whose book The Looting Machine documents how resource extraction across Africa, from oil and diamonds to gold and coltan, has repeatedly enriched multinational corporations, corrupt political elites, and international financial networks while leaving resource-producing populations impoverished and often subject to violent conflict fueled by competition over resource revenue. His investigative reporting traces the specific financial networks, shell companies, and complicit intermediaries that enable this extraction and the associated flight of wealth out of Africa into international financial centers and tax havens. He draws on extensive fieldwork and financial documentation to name specific corporate and political actors involved in these networks, an approach that has led to significant legal challenges against his reporting. His work situates African resource extraction within a global financial architecture that facilitates and often actively enables this looting. He continues investigative reporting on corruption, resource extraction, and illicit finance for the Financial Times.
