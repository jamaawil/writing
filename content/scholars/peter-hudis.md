Peter Hudis is an American scholar working in the Marxist-humanist tradition associated with Raya Dunayevskaya. His biography "Frantz Fanon: Philosopher of the Barricades" reframes Fanon primarily as a systematic philosopher rather than solely as a psychiatrist or revolutionary polemicist, tracing the philosophical architecture underlying works like "The Wretched of the Earth." He has also served as a general editor of the complete works of Rosa Luxemburg, contributing to renewed scholarly attention to her economic and political thought. His work situates anticolonial and revolutionary thinkers within longer traditions of Marxist humanism and dialectical philosophy. He continues to be an active voice in debates over the philosophical legacy of Fanon and Marxist theory more broadly.
