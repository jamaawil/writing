---
title: "Praxis"
slug: "praxis"
tagline: "(n.) knowledge made actionable through embodied practice"
---

Drawn from Aristotle and later developed by Marxist and critical theory traditions, *praxis* names the integration of theory and practice — not theory applied to practice, but theory and practice fused in action. Praxis is distinguished from both *theoria* (pure contemplation) and *poiesis* (making things according to a plan). It describes action that is itself the site of understanding.

In practical terms: you do not fully understand a concept until you have applied it in conditions of genuine uncertainty. Reading about negotiation is *theoria*. Practicing scripted negotiation is *poiesis*. Negotiating a real deal where the outcome matters and adapting in real time is *praxis*.

The concept is useful as a corrective to purely theoretical education, which can produce graduates who can describe a skill without being able to perform it, and to purely practical training, which can produce competent technicians who cannot explain or adapt what they do.
