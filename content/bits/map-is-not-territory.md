---
title: The Map Is Not the Territory
date: 2025-04-22
source: Alfred Korzybski / Naval Ravikant
---

Every model, framework, or mental map is a simplification of reality — not reality itself. The danger comes when we mistake our models for the truth, stop questioning them, and let them filter out data that contradicts them.

The most intellectually dangerous thing you can do is become too confident in your current map of the world.
