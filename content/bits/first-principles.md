---
title: First Principles Thinking
date: 2025-02-14
source: Aristotle / Elon Musk
---

Instead of reasoning by analogy — "this is how it's always been done" — first principles thinking means breaking a problem down to its most fundamental truths and rebuilding your solution from the ground up.

Analogy thinking is faster. First principles thinking is more powerful. The rarest skill is knowing which one the moment calls for.
