---
title: Compounding Knowledge
date: 2025-06-10
source: James Clear — Atomic Habits
---

Knowledge compounds just like money. A 1% improvement in understanding something each day results in being 37 times better over a year. Most people overestimate what they can learn in a week and massively underestimate what they can absorb in a decade of consistent, deliberate study.

The key is building systems that make learning automatic — not relying on willpower or inspiration.
