---
title: The Power of Negative Space
date: 2024-11-30
source: Gestalt Psychology / Tim Ferriss
---

What you choose not to do defines you as much as what you do. In design, negative space — the empty areas around objects — is what gives the composition room to breathe and the main subject its power.

The same principle applies to knowledge work, relationships, and strategy. Saying no creates the negative space that makes your yeses meaningful.
